What a StockIQ report answers

Search any listed company and get a full research report, built by Python maths first and explained in plain English second.

Is the business growing?

Revenue and profit growth, 3- and 5-year CAGR, segment mix and the margin trend behind them.

Is it financially healthy?

Debt to equity, interest cover, current ratio, and whether reported profit is actually turning into cash.

How expensive is it?

P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA and PEG, compared with the company's own history and with its peers.

What could go wrong?

A ranked risk dashboard, the bear case, and the specific conditions that would break the investment thesis.

No blind buy or sell signals. StockIQ never prints BUY, SELL or HOLD. Instead it shows what would make a stock more attractive, what would argue for waiting, and what should make you cautious, so you can form and test your own view.
